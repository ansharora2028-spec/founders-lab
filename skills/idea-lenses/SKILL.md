# venture idea lenses

Every idea comes from one lens applied to at least one cited signal (`L<nn>` from `01-local-market.md` or `S<nn>` from `02-signals.md`). An idea with no signal is not an idea; it is a guess. Spread ideas across lenses: in standard and deep modes, no lens may produce more than a third of the ideas.

For each lens: the question to ask, what a good output looks like, and the trap to avoid.

## 1. Unbundle
- **Question:** Which bloated incumbent product forces a segment to pay for 20 features to use 2?
- **Look for:** complaints about price, complexity or bloat in reviews and forums; a segment that only uses one workflow.
- **Good output:** a focused product for one segment and one job, priced below the incumbent.
- **Trap:** the "one feature" is already free inside the incumbent, so nobody switches.

## 2. Transplant
- **Question:** What model is proven (with revenue) in another geography or segment and absent or weak in the target market?
- **Look for:** funded or profitable companies elsewhere; local searches for the same thing with no good local result.
- **Good output:** the proven model adapted to local price points, language, payment habits and regulation.
- **Trap:** the model worked elsewhere because of a condition the target market lacks (purchasing power, infrastructure, regulation, culture). Name the enabling conditions and check them.

## 3. Boring B2B
- **Question:** What unsexy, high-pain operational problem do small businesses in the target market pay for today with time, staff or spreadsheets?
- **Look for:** owner-operator forums, trade associations, job posts for manual roles, compliance deadlines.
- **Good output:** a narrow tool or service that replaces a paid manual process, sold to a reachable list of businesses.
- **Trap:** the contract says B2C only (then this lens is skipped), or the sales cycle exceeds the time-to-first-revenue constraint.

## 4. Unfair advantage
- **Question:** What can *this* user execute better or cheaper than a random competent founder, given their stated advantages?
- **Look for:** the contract's unfair-advantages section; industry access, communities, distribution, family businesses, rare skills.
- **Good output:** an idea whose CAC or delivery cost is materially lower because of the advantage.
- **Trap:** inventing advantages the user did not state. If the user stated none, this lens produces nothing, and you say so.

## 5. Contrarian
- **Question:** What consensus belief about this market is probably wrong, and what would you build if it is?
- **Look for:** signals that contradict what most competitors assume (pricing, channel, customer, format).
- **Good output:** an idea whose bet is explicit: "Consensus says X. Evidence Y suggests not-X in this segment."
- **Trap:** contrarian for its own sake, with no signal behind the inversion.

## 6. Cheap-now
- **Question:** What became 10x cheaper or newly possible in the last 12–24 months (models, APIs, hardware, regulation, payment rails), and what does that unlock here?
- **Look for:** signal-scout's "newly cheap or possible technology" section.
- **Good output:** a product whose unit economics only work at the new cost, with the old and new cost both sourced.
- **Trap:** a thin wrapper that the platform owner will ship as a feature. Name the platform dependency.

## Path (a) — the user's own idea
When the user arrived with a specific idea:
- the user's original idea is always `I01`, lens `user-original`, stated as faithfully as possible. It always goes through Screen;
- up to 3 variants follow, lens `user-variant`, each changing one thing (segment, channel, price model, or geography) and naming which lens inspired the change.

## Path (b) — a space or industry
Apply the lenses inside the stated space only. Note in generation notes if a lens could not produce an idea inside the space.

## Ledger check
Before finalising, compare every idea with the ledger's dead ideas for a similar contract and location. If an idea resembles a dead one, either drop it or state the material change since that run (new signal, new cost, new regulation, different segment) in `material change vs ledger`.
