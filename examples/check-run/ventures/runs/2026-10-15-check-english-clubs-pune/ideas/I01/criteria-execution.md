## Criteria

### KE1
- dies unless: a plan to run enough weekend groups to cover costs (the ~35-50 paying students the raw idea implies, i.e. about 3 groups) fits inside the user's HARD limits of 10 hours/week (K2, outside the full-time job, K9) and ₹1,00,000 total spend (K1, K5), with the first rupee collected within 3 months (K6).
- threshold: total founder hours at the steady-state size (sales and WhatsApp, café booking, payments and attendance admin, preparing topics, plus every session the founder personally leads, including travel) of 10 hours/week or less; cumulative spend to first month-3 of ₹60,000 or less (leaving at least ₹40,000 reserve); first paid member within 90 days of 2026-10-15. Hours that depend on a hired facilitator count only if the facilitator's cost is inside the ₹1,00,000.
- evidence that satisfies it: a week-by-week hours table with session length and count taken from named comparable Pune operators or Toastmasters-style meeting formats; a cost table with sourced Pune freelancer/facilitator rates and café terms; no stretch of K2 (it is HARD).
- why it matters: weekend sessions are a fixed block of hours that grow with each added group, so the 10-hour cap may bind before the break-even student count is reached.

### KE2
- dies unless: at least 3 Pune cafés (or an equivalent venue type) are shown to host a recurring paid-group weekend session of about 90 minutes, 12-15 people, on terms the founder can afford, and without a platform or venue that can end the arrangement at will.
- threshold: venue cost per session of ₹1,000 or less in cash (or a minimum-spend model where members' own orders cover it, with that minimum-spend evidenced at or below ₹150 per head); at least 3 distinct venues; the arrangement must not need a written exclusivity or a revenue share above 20% of the ₹499 fee.
- evidence that satisfies it: a published café event/private-booking rate card, or listings of Pune cafés that host paid workshops/meetups with their fee or minimum spend, or a documented quote from cafés. An event listing alone, such as a free Luma meetup, does not show a rate for a paid group.
- why it matters: the café is the delivery channel and the only cost line the generator could not size; a café can raise its fee, drop weekend tables at peak hours, or object to a group occupying seats without sufficient orders.

### KE3
- dies unless: the legal and registration cost and effort of running paid, recurring group sessions for students in Pune, including those under 18, is shown to be both permissible and affordable within the contract.
- threshold: total registration, licence, insurance and compliance cost in year 1 of ₹10,000 or less; no licence or accreditation that takes longer than 60 days or that the user does not hold (K8 bars anything needing a licence they lack); a determination, from a primary source or professional, on (a) Maharashtra Shops and Establishments registration / PMC Gumasta for a café-hosted, home-run service, (b) GST registration trigger for services (the ₹20 lakh threshold is not expected to bind, but inter-state or platform supply can trigger it), (c) whether charging or hosting minors needs consent or safeguarding steps, and (d) liability if a student is hurt or harassed at a session.
- evidence that satisfies it: the Maharashtra Labour Department or PMC pages or a registered professional's written opinion; the GST portal rules; an insurance quote for event or public-liability cover in Pune.
- why it matters: the market scout found no primary-source answer (01 C21-C22), the user has low risk appetite (K5), and a safeguarding or liability incident with young adults in public venues is an unbudgeted loss.

### KE4
- dies unless: the likely response of incumbents and free substitutes does not remove the price position within the first 6 months of operation.
- threshold: evidence that none of these already offers, or can add at a marginal cost close to zero, an in-person or live group English-speaking session in Pune at ₹1,000/month or less: (a) Pune coaching institutes with weekend/demo batches, (b) open Toastmasters clubs (free to attend or low dues), (c) college English/communication societies and free WhatsApp/Telegram or Meetup groups, (d) SpeakX at ₹299/month or similar apps that could add live human sessions. At least the dues of Toastmasters clubs and the prices of at least 3 Pune institutes' shortest or weekend plans must be found.
- evidence that satisfies it: Toastmasters club dues from toastmasters.org or club pages; price pages or demo-batch pages of at least 3 Pune institutes; app feature pages showing whether live human or group sessions are offered in India; a count of free English clubs at the largest Pune colleges.
- why it matters: the scout found free and cheaper substitutes already present (01 L02, 02 S02), and ₹499 has no moat if an institute runs one cheap weekend batch or a college club does the same free.

### KE5
- dies unless: the delivery model keeps a positive contribution margin when the founder is not running every session, and it cannot be copied at no cost by a member or hired facilitator.
- threshold: facilitator cost at a sourced Pune market rate (not the founder's unpaid time) plus venue plus payment fees of 50% or less of ₹499 per student per month, at 10 paying students per group and 4 sessions per month; a retention assumption of no more than 50% of members renewing for month 2 must still leave the monthly cash margin positive after the ₹1,00,000 is partly spent on acquisition; a named barrier (brand, content, location, vetted facilitators) that stops a facilitator or the top members from starting the same group on WhatsApp and charging directly.
- evidence that satisfies it: sourced Pune freelancer / English tutor hourly rates (the scout has ₹702/hour for tutors, 01 C04); payment-gateway fee schedule (UPI/Razorpay or similar); retention figures from comparable subscription clubs or offline classes; evidence of how comparable community businesses handled facilitator or member poaching.
- why it matters: at ₹499 and 10 to 15 students a group, one paid hour of a facilitator at ₹702 consumes more than the per-session revenue share, so the margin depends on who leads and on repeat attendance, and success invites cheap copies.
