## Regulation and licensing
- [C33] Draft Maharashtra Private Coaching Centers (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2026 (draft dated 20 August 2026; not in force): covers academic coaching, tuition, instruction or guidance for "more than 25 students"; non-academic creative activities are excluded [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]
- [C34] The same draft says centres may not "enrol students below 13 years of age", may not run classes for more than five hours a day, must issue fee receipts, may not raise fees mid-course, and must refund a leaver pro rata within ten days [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]; [source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/no-classes-beyond-5-hours-maharashtra-drafts-law-to-regulate-private-coaching-centres-public-feedback-invited-till-sept-4]
- [C35] Draft penalties: minor violations ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh; repeat minor up to ₹10 lakh; major ₹10 lakh to ₹50 lakh; registration can be suspended or cancelled [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]
- [C36] Comments were open until 6 pm on 4 September 2026. On 8 September 2026 the Professional Teachers Association of Maharashtra (about 50,000 members) asked for the threshold to rise to more than 100 students and for the under-13 ban to be dropped, and announced a one-day shutdown on 11 September. Status after 8 September 2026 was not checked [source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/pune/pune-teachers-body-seeks-consultation-before-maharashtra-coaching-law-is-finalised]
- [C37] Shops and establishments: per a secondary source, a standalone tuition or coaching centre may need registration unless it is recognised by a university or board; sources disagree on the employee threshold and on the deadline (30 or 60 days) [source: https://www.greythr.com/wiki/acts/maharashtra-shops-commercial-establishments-act/; search snippet]. Verify with the Maharashtra labour department.
- [C38] GST: 2018 to 2022 Advance Rulings held that coaching which does not lead to a legally recognised qualification is not exempt. The rulings concerned institutes, and none directly covers a sole tutor [source: https://news.cleartax.in/ap-aar-coaching-classes-will-not-be-exempted-from-gst/5033/amp; search snippet]. One secondary source says coaching classes with turnover of ₹20 lakh or more must register [source: https://www.gconnect.in/gst-news/coaching-classes-to-pay-18-gst-students-bear-brunt.html; search snippet]; verify against current CGST rules.
- [C39] (national) Digital Personal Data Protection Rules: verifiable parental consent is needed before processing the data of anyone under 18; the sources say this applies to schools, colleges and edtech [source: https://ssrana.in/?p=45905; search snippet]
