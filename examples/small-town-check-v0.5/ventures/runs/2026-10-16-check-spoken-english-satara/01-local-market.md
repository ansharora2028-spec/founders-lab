## Regulation and licensing
- [C27] Maharashtra published a draft "Maharashtra Private Coaching Centers (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2026" on 2026-08-20. Objections were due 2026-09-04. Status after that date was not found. (state; draft, not law) [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]
- [C28] The draft covers centres giving "academic coaching, tuition, instruction or guidance" to more than 25 students for consideration; sports, dance, theatre, painting and yoga are excluded. Whether spoken English counts as "academic" was not stated in the summaries I read. (state; draft) [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]
- [C29] The draft bars enrolling students below 13. Class 6-7 students are typically 11-13 [ASSUMPTION: standard Indian school-age norms, class 6 entry about age 11]. If spoken English batches fall inside the Act, class 6 and most class 7 students could not legally be enrolled. (state; draft) [source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/no-classes-beyond-5-hours-maharashtra-drafts-law-to-regulate-private-coaching-centres-public-feedback-invited-till-sept-4]
- [C30] Draft duties for covered centres: online registration valid 3 years, 1 sq m per student, no basements, CCTV with one month of footage, separate boys' and girls' toilets, fire and building safety certification, fee receipts, pro-rata refunds within 10 days, fines of ₹1 lakh to ₹50 lakh. (state; draft) [source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/]
- [C31] Coaching teachers' consortium (SCCTI) asked that registration apply only above 100 students, and opposed the under-13 ban. This is lobbying, not a decision. (state) [source: https://news.careers360.com/maharashtra-coaching-centres-seek-one-time-registration-lower-space-norm-draft-regulation-law/amp, 2026-09-02]
- [C32] Interim: district inspection committees under the District Collector monitor coaching institutes in line with Supreme Court directions (minister's statement 2026-06-30). (state) [source: https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/maha-draft-legislation-underway-regulate-pre-primary-private-coaching-592.htm]
- [C33] Other registrations (shop and establishment, GST threshold, local trade licence for a rented room) were not researched; mark as unknown. [ASSUMPTION: a rented teaching room needs a municipal/Shops and Establishments registration; not verified]
