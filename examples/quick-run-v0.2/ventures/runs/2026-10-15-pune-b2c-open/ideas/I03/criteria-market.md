## Criteria
### KM1
- dies unless: there is demand evidence that tenants (not landlords or brokers) already pay for a tenant-side paid product in India: rental agreement drafting, move-in inspection report, or deposit-recovery help.
- threshold: at least 3 named providers operating in Pune or another Indian metro, each with a published tenant-facing price of at least ₹199 per use and a visible sign of paying customers (at least 100 public reviews or transactions, or disclosed revenue).
- evidence that satisfies it: provider pricing pages plus app-store or Google review counts, press or filings with revenue, each with URL and date. Free-only products and landlord-paid products do not count.
- why it matters: rule 3 requires demand evidence, and the raw idea rests on one anecdote (S08) with no proven tenant spend.

### KM2
- dies unless: tenant deposit or no-written-contract disputes are shown to be a recurring problem in Pune, not a single anecdote.
- threshold: at least 20 independently documented Pune tenant deposit-withholding or no-agreement disputes dated within the last 24 months (consumer commission orders, news, Reddit r/pune, complaint sites), or one Pune/Maharashtra survey showing at least 30% of PG or flat tenants have no written agreement.
- evidence that satisfies it: list of dated cases or threads with URLs, or a survey or official report with sample size and geography stated.
- why it matters: the pain evidence today is one complaint (S08), which does not meet the recurrence bar.

### KM3
- dies unless: the ₹399 kit does something that free substitutes do not.
- threshold: a check of the top 10 results for "rent agreement format", "deposit refund letter" and "move-in checklist" (English, Hindi, Marathi) plus the tenant features of NoBroker, Housing.com, MagicBricks and at least 2 others shows no free source that provides all three components (agreement, condition report, deposit-return demand) in at least one of Hindi or Marathi.
- evidence that satisfies it: dated screenshots or URL list of the search and app review, with each substitute's coverage marked per component.
- why it matters: free templates, lawyers and the state's online registration are the incumbents; "do nothing" and a free download are the main competitors.

### KM4
- dies unless: the reachable Pune market is large enough to hit the contract's first-revenue window and a break-even volume.
- threshold: evidence of at least 300,000 rented households plus PG or hostel residents in Pune (city or urban agglomeration), and at least 5 identifiable Pune tenant communities (student, IT-park, PG groups) with a combined stated membership of at least 20,000, from which about 100 kits at ₹399 (₹39,900 revenue) is plausible within 3 months.
- evidence that satisfies it: Census/NSS housing tables or a credible Pune rental market report, plus public membership counts of the named communities.
- why it matters: the founder has 10 hours per week and 3 months (K2, K6), so the reachable pool must be countable, not assumed.

### KM5
- dies unless: the graveyard of tenant-side paid products in India does not show low tenant willingness to pay as the cause of death.
- threshold: fewer than 2 identified Indian startups (any city) that sold a one-time tenant-side document, inspection or deposit-recovery product and shut down or pivoted to landlord, broker or B2B customers, with failure causes sourced.
- evidence that satisfies it: a documented graveyard search (Tracxn, Crunchbase, CB Insights, press post-mortems) that names the companies and causes, with URLs and dates. If 2 or more are found and low tenant willingness to pay is a cause, the criterion is not met.
- why it matters: Pune rental tech is landlord-side by default (S07), which is consistent with tenants not being a paying segment.
