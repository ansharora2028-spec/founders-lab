## Regulation and licensing
- [C20] Maharashtra Shops and Establishments registration is online via the state labour department; vendor sources say home-based and freelance operations can register using a residential address and that filing should be within 30 days of starting. They disagree on whether under-10-worker businesses need full registration or only an intimation. Not verified against the official portal. [source: https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/shop-and-establishment-registration-for-shops-offices-and-online-businesses]
- [C21] GST registration thresholds cited by a vendor: ₹40 lakh turnover for goods and ₹20 lakh for services. (national; a vendor source, verify) [source: https://www.deskera.com/blog/form-b-maharashtra/]
- [C22] A municipal trade licence is separate from Gumasta registration and may also be needed (vendor claim). [source: https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/services/gumasta-license-maharashtra]
- [C23] The DPDP Rules were notified on 2025-11-13. Core notice and consent duties for consumer apps apply from about 2027-05-13, 18 months after notification. (national) [source: https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708164/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified, 2025-11-13]
- reasoning: no Pune-specific permit rules for category-level activity (e.g. pet boarding, home services, tutoring) were researched.
