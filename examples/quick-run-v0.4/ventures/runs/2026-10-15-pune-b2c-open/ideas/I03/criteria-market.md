## Criteria
### KM1
- dies unless: there is demand evidence that non-app, independent home deep-cleaning providers in Pune already have paying customers at a 1 BHK deep-clean price of at least ₹2,500
- threshold: at least 3 distinct independent Pune providers (not Urban Company, Snabbit or Pronto), each with a published 1 BHK deep-clean price of ₹2,500 or more AND at least 50 dated customer reviews on Google Maps, Justdial or a similar listing
- evidence that satisfies it: listing pages or price sheets with review counts and review dates (dated within the last 12 months), opened and cited by URL
- why it matters: the only demand proof in 01 is Urban Company (D05); there is no evidence yet that anyone outside the dominant app earns revenue in Pune at this price

### KM2
- dies unless: the going Pune price for a comparable 1 BHK deep clean supports the idea's roughly ₹3,000 price with a spread over the team cost of about ₹2,200
- threshold: median price across at least 5 Pune listings for a comparable 1 BHK deep clean (scope stated) is at least ₹3,000, and at least 3 Pune teams or vendors publish or quote a per-job rate at or below ₹2,200 for the same scope
- evidence that satisfies it: published price lists or dated quotes from Pune providers (customer-side and cleaner-side), with the scope of work in each
- why it matters: the stated margin is about ₹700 a job; a ₹300–500 price gap or a higher team rate removes it

### KM3
- dies unless: the quality gap is real in Pune and not already closed by incumbents
- threshold: at least 20 dated complaints from Pune customers within the last 12 months about skipped areas, rushed work or missing re-clean, across at least 2 platforms or at least 5 localities; AND of at least 5 Pune providers checked (including Urban Company), no more than 2 publicly offer a free re-clean or photo proof of work
- evidence that satisfies it: Google Maps, Urban Company locality pages, Justdial or consumer-forum complaints with dates; provider terms or help pages showing re-clean, redo or photo policies
- why it matters: the idea's only differentiator is a photo checklist plus re-clean guarantee; if incumbents already offer it, there is no gap

### KM4
- dies unless: there is measurable Pune demand for the service that the founder can reach inside 3 months at an affordable acquisition cost
- threshold: combined Pune monthly search volume of at least 1,500 for deep-cleaning terms (English, Hindi or Marathi variants), AND evidence of a paid or listing channel with a cost per lead at or below ₹250 (which at a stated 40% lead-to-booking rate is about ₹600 per booking, the idea's own CAC placeholder)
- evidence that satisfies it: Google Keyword Planner or equivalent tool output (screenshot or cited page), and published or quoted CPC and listing-lead prices for Pune home-service categories
- why it matters: the first job barely breaks even at a ₹600 CAC, so both demand volume and the cost to reach it must hold

### KM5
- dies unless: repeat or referral demand exists for comparable home-cleaning services, since the first job does not pay back
- threshold: at least one documented comparable home-cleaning service in an Indian city reporting a repeat-booking or referral rate of at least 25% of customers within 6 months; AND the graveyard check finds no more than 1 documented Indian home-cleaning or home-services startup that closed mainly because customer acquisition cost exceeded per-job margin, unless the idea evidences a specific difference from it
- evidence that satisfies it: company statements, funding-news interviews or investor reports giving repeat rates; shutdown reports or founder post-mortems with dated cause of failure
- why it matters: the idea's own money math depends on repeat bookings, and thin-margin home-services marketplaces are a known failure pattern that 01 did not cover for Pune (01 found no Pune consumer-startup failure data)
