## Regulation and licensing
- [C35a] Sources agree the GST registration threshold is Rs 40 lakh for goods and Rs 20 lakh for services, and that e-commerce sellers and inter-state suppliers must register regardless of turnover. These are consultancy pages, not government text. (national rule) [source: https://www.kanakkupillai.com/gst-registration-online-in-pune]
- [C36] Consultancy pages say Shop Act (Gumasta) registration applies to fixed-premises businesses in Pune, including home-based ones, and is filed online. They disagree on fees and on whether firms with fewer than 10 employees only file an intimation. No official PMC or Labour Department page was seen. [source: https://www.patronaccounting.com/shop-act-registration/pune, 2026]
- [C37] The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified on 2025-11-14, with phased compliance of up to about 18 months. They apply to small and large entities that process digital personal data. (national) [source: https://www.seqrite.com/blog/what-is-the-dpdp-act-2025-key-rules-compliance-requirements-and-business-impact/]
- [C38] Education Ministry guidelines of January 2024 call for pro-rata refunds from coaching institutes, and the National Consumer Helpline (1915) handles refund requests. (national) [source: https://righttoinformation.wiki/practical-guides/coaching-institute-refund-batch-cancellation-poor-service]
- [C39] Platform orders on Swiggy and Zomato carry 5% GST collected by the platform under Section 9(5), and cloud kitchens selling through them must register for GST. (national rule) [source: https://www.patronaccounting.com/gst-returns-for-restaurants-food-businesses/pune]
- ASSUMPTION: food, pet-medical and childcare ideas would fall under K8 (food-safety and medical licences), and creche or pre-school ideas likely need state registration. This was not researched.
